MANUSCRIPT EQUATIONS (copy with Microsoft Word Equation)

(1) General regression objective:
    f_hat = arg min_f [ (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n L(y_i, f(x_i)) + lambda Omega(f) ]

(2) Standard daily growing degree days (reference formula):
    GDD_d = max( ((T_max,d + T_min,d)/2) - T_base, 0 )

(3) Standard accumulated growing degree days:
    AGDD_y = sum_{d=1}^{D_y} GDD_d

(4) Approximation actually used in this study (annual thermal-excess proxy):
    GDD_proxy,y = max(T_mean,y - T_base, 0), T_base = 10 degrees C

(5) Training-fitted year normalization and transforms:
    Year_norm = (Year - Year_min,train)/(Year_max,train - Year_min,train)
    Year_sin = sin(2*pi*Year_norm)
    Year_cos = cos(2*pi*Year_norm)

(6) XGBoost regularization:
    Omega(f_k) = gamma*T_k + (lambda/2) sum_{j=1}^{T_k} w_kj^2

Important: GDD_proxy is not accumulated daily GDD; fc1_pet is a temperature-based PET proxy, not the full monthly Thornthwaite procedure.
